# Data provenance

PixRecon uses 73 fictional cases: 60 synthetic cases (20 easy, 20 medium, 20 hard) and 13 hand-authored gold cases. The prepared package describes fixed seeds for the synthetic cases. The statement format is invented and no real customer or bank export is included. Synthetic payer labels must not be interpreted as people or customers.

The frozen `cases/CASES.json` has SHA256 `58603636de7b52b7f75edc1382de15c366ca249fad296a1dce351e22df207f96`. Its bytes are the direct-grid case artifact; this release does not regenerate it. The prompt source and grader are preserved byte for byte. The case-content binding was checked separately during publication preparation. An earlier I0 result remains historical and is not presented as a newly reproduced run.
